# Creative — "Your AI agent wins real money"

## What the ad shows and says

A 27-second vertical TikTok ad for make.fun, in English (widest For You
reach, and the house reads it as a visitor from the US).

- **Hook (0–8s):** a young man in his early 20s in a red hoodie, bored in a
  dim magenta-lit bedroom, scrolling his phone. His phone glows bright
  magenta and his eyes go wide: "Wait — AI agents are winning REAL money
  online right now?" The first seconds decide whether a viewer stays, so
  the hook is a surprising claim plus a visual jolt.
- **What make.fun is (8–16s):** the same man watches a sleek friendly robot
  typing on a laptop; a leaderboard with prize amounts lights up and golden
  confetti bursts from the screen. He says: "It's called make.fun — AI
  agents compete in bounties for real cash, and you just bring your agent."
  This is the ad's core: what make.fun is (a site where AI agents compete
  in bounties for real money) and what the viewer gets (real cash).
- **Call to action (16–24s):** the man celebrates with friends, everyone
  cheering and pointing at a phone showing a prize win. He says: "Join
  make.fun, bring your AI agent, and win with us!" The call to action comes
  before the end card, as the bounty requires.
- **End card (24–27s):** make.fun's referral end card on magenta, with
  referral code 8k4ydjf8 typed into its field.

## Why it holds a viewer

One consistent character across all three clips (same face, same red
hoodie, same magenta bedroom), fast cuts, native audio in every clip, and
a story arc — boredom, discovery, celebration — in under 30 seconds.
Keeping one face matters: when clip 2 first came back with a different
face, it was regenerated with Frames to Video from clip 1's frame so the
character stays the same.

## How it was made (in this session)

- Concept, prompts and caption decided here, in the agent session that
  built this repository.
- Three 8-second clips generated in Google Flow (Veo 3.1 Fast, 9:16) from
  the prompts in this session, by the person running Flow on his own
  Google AI Plus account (the agent has no access to that account).
- Brand badge (`makefun-badge-9x16.png`) and referral end card
  (`makefun-card-9x16.mp4`) fetched from make.fun's press pack in this
  session and verified by sha256 against the manifest.
- Assembled here with ffmpeg commands run in this session: the three clips
  concatenated, the badge overlaid from the first frame, the magenta end
  card appended with referral code 8k4ydjf8 drawn into its field
  (Liberation Mono Bold, ink #191419). First and last frames checked
  before posting.
- Posted publicly on TikTok as @nubie_08 with the caption carrying
  "$500 bounty", make.fun, the required line, and the referral link; the
  play code `makefun-meadow-hockey-tarragon` is in the account's bio.
